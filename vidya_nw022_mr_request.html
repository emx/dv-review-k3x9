काय करायचे

खालील इंग्रजी हा स्रोत आहे. प्रत्येक परिच्छेदाखाली त्याचे मराठी रूप लिहा — गंभीर, संक्षिप्त, अलंकाराशिवाय. इंग्रजीत जे नाही ते मराठीत आणू नका. कुलकर्णी किंवा W8 यांचा उल्लेख करू नका. जुनी सत्र-२ मराठी प्रस्तावना बदलायची नाही; हे नवीन रूपांतरण आहे.

The task

The English below is the source of record (v2). Write a Marathi rendering under each paragraph, in the same register: grave, short, without flourish. Do not add a claim the English does not make. Do not mention Kulkarni or W8. Do not edit the old Session-2 Marathi still in the book — this is a new rendering from this source.

1. This volume sets out the verse text of the Dāsbodh of Samarth Ramdas — twenty dashaks, two hundred samāsa — as it stands in the Shridhar Sārtha print, freed of everything that is not the poem.

2. The print is a sārtha: verse carried inside commentary. Here the commentary of Shri Shridhar Swami is set aside by selection and only the mūla — the ovī text itself — is kept. Nothing else is altered. The working rule of this edition admits no exception: the book is never changed to fit the instrument.

3. The text was not retyped. The print's own digital layer, damaged by the encoding it was born with, was recovered whole — each broken glyph traced back to the character it stood for through a fixed table: deterministic, repeatable, checkable.

4. Recovery invites doubt, so the recovered text was made to earn trust. It was read against independent witnesses of the Dāsbodh — the Wikisource lineage and other digital editions — and where the witnesses parted ways, the disputed sites were put to a bilingual reader against the printed page itself, in measured sessions with planted controls, so that even the checking was checked.

5. Every verse printed here is tied, byte for byte, to a pinned master text recorded in the colophon; presentation is never allowed to rewrite the pāṭha. Where sources agree, the reading stands silently; every remaining doubt is kept visible in the apparatus.

6. This edition does not claim perfection. It claims a traceable reading: what was printed, why, and what remains open can each be checked — the full accounting stands in the colophon at the end of this volume.
